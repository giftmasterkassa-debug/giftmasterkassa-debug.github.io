Privacy Policy — reklama bot

Gift Master · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

«reklama bot» is an internal application operated by Gift Master. It is used only with Gmail accounts that belong to Gift Master. It is not offered to the public.

What Google user data the app accesses

  • Send email (gmail.send): to send business offer emails from the company's own Gmail account.
  • Read email (gmail.readonly): to read recent messages in the same inbox in order to detect replies, delivery failures (bounces) and unsubscribe requests, so that the app stops writing to people who do not want to receive messages.

How the data is used and stored

The app stores only what it needs to work: recipient email addresses, the date a message was sent, and whether the recipient replied, unsubscribed or the message bounced. This information is kept on the company's private server and is used only to run the company's own mailings.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Unsubscribe and deletion

If you received an email from Gift Master and do not want more, reply «STOP» and your address will be excluded from future mailings. To ask for your data to be deleted, write to the contact address below. The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
